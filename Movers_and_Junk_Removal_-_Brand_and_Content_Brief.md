## Pain Points (what customers hate about other movers)

**1. Their stuff gets broken**
Broken furniture, holes in walls, scratched floors, damaged TVs, and then nobody takes responsibility.
*Customer words:* broke, damaged, scratched, dented, dropped, careless, "no apology," "didn't even tell us"

**2. Surprise charges**
The price on moving day doesn't match the quote. Fees show up that nobody mentioned.
*Customer words:* overcharged, hidden fees, "double the quote," bait and switch, "sneak it in at the end," misleading, rip off

**3. Nobody calls back**
Rude office staff, hang-ups, mixed answers, no follow-up.
*Customer words:* rude, hung up, runaround, "never heard back," unprofessional, "nothing but a headache"

**4. Slow crews on the clock**
Standing around, long breaks, dragging out the job.
*Customer words:* slow, standing around, "on my dime," wasting time, "took all day," lazy

**5. Late or no-show**
Late arrivals, last-minute cancellations, no heads-up.
*Customer words:* late, canceled, no call, "didn't even show up," "had to scramble"

**6. Job left half done**
Boxes dumped in the garage, furniture not put back together, customers doing the lifting themselves.
*Customer words:* "left everything in the garage," "had to finish it myself," "we ended up helping," wrong rooms

**7. Stressful and pushy**
Tip pressure, attitude, feeling disrespected in your own home.
*Customer words:* stressful, nightmare, pushy, attitude, disrespectful, "worst experience"
