## 🎯 Order of Importance

If you want to launch with images gradually:

1. **MUST HAVE (Essential)**
   - `hero.jpg` - Hero image
   - `before.jpg` + `after.jpg` - Before/After slider
   - `gallery-1.jpg` through `gallery-8.jpg` - Gallery showcase

2. **HIGHLY RECOMMENDED**
   - Service cards (5 images)
   - Service showcase (3 images)

3. **NICE TO HAVE**
   - How It Works timeline (4 images)
   - Final CTA background (1 image)
